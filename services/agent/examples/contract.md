# Marketplace web app contract

## Authentication
1. Users must be able to log in at /login with an email and password.
2. An invalid password must show an error message and keep the user on the login page.

## Marketplace
- Providers can create a listing with a title, description and price at /listings/new.
- The marketplace page at /marketplace must show every published listing with its title and price.

## Milestones
- The milestone page at /milestones must show each milestone with its status (pending, in review or approved).
- A client may approve a milestone in review, and its status must change to approved.

## Payment
- The client shall pay each approved milestone invoice within 30 days of approval.
